# Memento: Hyperpersonalized Learning App

## Overview

Memento is your ultimate AI-powered learning companion, transforming raw knowledge into deeply personalized, multimedia experiences. Inspired by tools like Notebook LM, Memento lets you upload diverse data sources—text files, PDFs, articles, or even scraped web content—and instantly interact with them through intelligent chat interfaces. Whether you're a student cramming for exams, a professional upskilling on the go, or a lifelong learner exploring passions, Memento adapts to your style, pace, and preferences. Upload your notes on the commute, and by the time you arrive, you'll have interactive audio overviews, debate simulations, or even a custom documentary ready to dive into. With seamless integration of voice cloning, immersive simulations, and automated content generation, Memento turns passive information into active, memorable mastery.

## Core Features

### Intelligent Source Management & Discovery

- **Upload & Interact**: Drag-and-drop your textual data, PDFs, or partial notes into Memento's central hub. Chat directly with your sources—like querying a PDF for key insights or cross-referencing multiple documents in natural language.
- **Auto-Discovery Engine**: Memento proactively searches, scrapes, and downloads relevant materials from the web or curated databases. Input a topic like "quantum computing basics," and it fetches articles, papers, and videos tailored to your skill level.
- **Expanded Deep Research**: Built-in web browsing and semantic search tools dive deeper, pulling in diverse sources (academic journals, expert blogs, real-time news) while citing them transparently for trust and verification.

### Multimedia Generation Suite

- **Audio Mastery**: Generate podcasts, summaries, or full audiobooks in multiple languages. Choose lengths from bite-sized 10-minute overviews to immersive 6-hour deep dives. Use your cloned voice for narration—upload a short sample, and Memento replicates it authentically for a personal touch.
- **Video Creator**: Produce polished video reports from 20 minutes to full-hour sessions. Auto-generate visuals, animations, and subtitles synced to your content. Export as shareable files or stream directly in-app.
- **Handbooks & Printables**: Prefer tangible formats? One-click conversion to customizable handbooks—50-page quick guides or 200-page comprehensive manuals—with embedded quizzes, diagrams, and hyperlinks.
- **Flashcards & Quizzes**: Instant creation of adaptive flashcards and gamified quizzes. Memento tracks your performance and refines them over time for spaced repetition.

### Immersive Interaction Modes

- **AI Podcast Studio**: Launch a multi-participant podcast with AI co-hosts. A dynamic window opens featuring your camera feed alongside 3-4 AI avatars (experts, peers, or characters). Discuss topics in real-time—they respond, debate, and improvise based on your uploads. Record sessions, edit highlights, or export as audio/video episodes. It's collaborative learning that feels alive and social.
- **AI Debate Room**: Step into a virtual roundtable. Select a topic, and Memento spawns 3-4 expert personas (e.g., a philosopher, scientist, and skeptic) who debate in real-time. Jump in with voice or text, pause for clarifications, or request consensus summaries. It's like eavesdropping on a TED panel—on demand, anytime.
- **Instant Documentary Mode**: Hit one button to alchemize your research notes into a cinematic short film. Memento crafts engaging visuals (stock footage, AI-generated graphics), voiceover narration (in your cloned voice), smooth transitions, and subtitles. In minutes, your scattered ideas become a 5-15 minute professional documentary—perfect for presentations or personal reflection.
- **Reality Mode: Immersive Tutor**: Activate this for a lifelike tutoring session. Your AI tutor listens to voice responses, poses probing questions, provides instant feedback, and dynamically adjusts the curriculum. It's not scripted—it's adaptive, like a one-on-one class with a world-class mentor who knows your blind spots.

### Productivity & Personalization Tools

- **Rapid Course Builder**: Upload any materials, and with a single click, Memento assembles a complete interactive course. It generates video modules, embedded quizzes, progress trackers, and summary flashcards. Customize branding, then share via links, export to LMS platforms, or publish to your network—empowering you to teach what you've learned.
- **Habit Tracker**: Built-in streaks, reminders, and analytics to build consistent learning routines. Set goals like "daily 20-min audio review," and Memento nudges you with personalized motivational audio clips in your voice.
- **Voice Cloning Integration**: Across all audio/video features, opt-in to clone your voice for hyper-personalization. It makes content feel like an extension of yourself—narrating your own breakthroughs or debating in your tone.
