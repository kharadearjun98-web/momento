Memento extends LunarTech Edge from a smart LMS into a truly living learning stack. It ingests institutional content and each learner’s own materials, applies advanced retrieval-augmented generation and multimodal synthesis, and produces tailor-made podcasts, documentaries, flashcards, debates, and micro-courses aligned with that learner’s profile, goals, and constraints established by Luna. Unlike existing tools that offer generic summaries or rigid assistants, Memento operates as a personal knowledge studio: autonomous agents curate sources, generate validated learning assets, and feed back fine-grained performance and preference signals into the platform. This creates a continuous personalization loop where every learner, in any domain, receives formats, explanations, and pathways engineered specifically for how they think, remember, and live.
